# WO-0007 — Spec defects (acceptance criteria not literally met)

## AC2 — "for 20 random symbols the materialised close on the last Polygon day equals the Polygon raw close exactly"
- **Measured:** 17 of 20 in the seeded sample; the three misses (CA, BBT, ENPH) have no published bar on their last Polygon day because that day is after their final membership end. Population: 263,908 of 312,025 Polygon rows identical, 48 within float32 noise (relative 5.1e-8) after a flag fallback to yfinance, 48,069 deliberately not published.
- **Cause:** AMD-0018 §2F (later than the WO text) bounds each symbol's bars by its own listing life, and AMD-0018 §2A withholds quarantined and flagged days. The AC predates both. This is a conflict between an acceptance criterion and a later binding Amendment, not an external data fact; it is handled by analogy with standing ruling 7 (measure, name the shortfall beside the aggregate, merge under the pre-committed acceptance) and is **flagged to the Architect for confirmation**.
- **Hard guards (any one means stop):** touches a critical invariant — no (CI-9 held: `bars_raw` untouched; CI-2 continuity preserved by `ticker_renames.yaml`); the fix would change behaviour rather than report — the remedy is to reword AC2 ("for symbols with a published bar on their last in-membership Polygon day"), no code change; weakens a non-vacuity requirement — no.
- **Suggested wording:** AC2 → "For 20 random symbols with a published bar on a Polygon day, the published close equals the Polygon close; report the count of Polygon rows not published and the reason."
