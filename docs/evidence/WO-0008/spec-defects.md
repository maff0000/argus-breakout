# WO-0008 — Spec defects (acceptance criteria not literally met)

## AC1 — "`argus ibkr reconcile` completes with `breach_rate < 0.01`"
- **Measured (live, 20 members, 5 sessions, 2026-10-13..2026-10-19):** 200 comparisons, 101 breaches, breach_rate 0.505. **Close: 1 breach in 100 comparisons (0.010; max |diff| 2.09%, SKYD).** **Volume: 100 breaches in 100 comparisons; median ours/theirs 2.03×, p5–p95 1.35–2.78×.** Even the close-only rate is 0.010, which is not `< 0.01` by the letter.
- **Cause (external data fact):** IBKR's daily TRADES volume on the paper gateway is roughly half of the consolidated volume that Polygon (and yfinance, to 0.03%) report for the same days, in shares not lots. A 10% volume tolerance cannot be met against that source; the discrepancy is systematic across all 20 symbols, not a defect in the comparison. The close comparison, the purpose of the reconciliation, agrees.
- **Standing ruling 7 applies; hard guards checked:** touches a critical invariant — no (nothing writes `bars`, `bars_raw`, `splits`, `dividends`; CI-9 held); the fix would change behaviour rather than report — **the remedy considered (a wider or ratio-based volume tolerance, or dropping the volume field) would change behaviour, so it is NOT applied here and is filed for the Architect**; weakens a non-vacuity requirement — no (tolerance boundary tests unchanged).
- **Consequence to flag:** WO-0009's nightly gate on `breach_rate` would trip every night if it reads the aggregate rate as specified. Recommend the Architect rule on (a) a per-field rate, close-only, as the gate; (b) volume reconciled as a ratio band against a stored per-source expectation, or recorded but not gated.
